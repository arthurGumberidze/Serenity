# UI04 validation — management workspaces and professional work rules

Date: 2026-10-31

Unity: 6000.6.4f1

Delivery: `Builds/UI04/Serenity.exe`

## Delivered scope

- Settlement overview now exposes workforce, resources/storage and production summaries; People has a searched,
  virtualized resident roster; Work Groups exposes profession, allowed work, shift, commander, membership,
  reassignment, deletion-with-replacement, priority and queues.
- Construction remains a world-visible lower drawer with category navigation and no overlap at 1280x720@125%.
  Research is a categorized dependency view (survival, production, settlement and society).
- `ProfessionGroupService` is the canonical transaction boundary for starting/new-adult/recruit reconciliation,
  profession-aligned reassignment and deletion. Its reconciliation is count/birthday gated and has no per-NPC
  `Update`.
- Grouped jobs can only be assigned to members of the required group. `Срочно` interrupts only an eligible member
  at the scheduler's safe boundary, keeps progress, and orders the urgent job first with prior work second.
  Work membership, queue order and stable IDs continue through the existing save and tier paths.

## Attempt diagnoses

- The first two PlayMode attempts reached the workspace assertions but used a point inside the intentionally
  interactive lower construction drawer as a "free world" probe. The unstable input-frame assertion was removed;
  compact geometry is asserted in PlayMode and usable-world behavior is asserted by the exact Player after idle
  frames. The final PlayMode passed.
- The first affected EditMode run exposed three pre-UI04 expectations: interrupted work was expected to become
  unassigned, saves expected ten rather than eleven professional groups, and empty builder groups could borrow a
  general worker. Tests were updated to the approved UI04 behavior and the product fallback was removed; 58 affected
  cases then passed.
- The first build attempt correctly failed the immutable-provenance guard because unrelated WM15/vendor assets
  changed during the build. A stable-source retry passed, and the final post-layout build also passed.
- Local visual review found the construction category row overlapping the bottom 34 pixels of the catalog. The
  drawer was lifted above that row, rebuilt, rerun and reviewed before delivery.

No UI05 dynasty/events/army implementation is included. The only reversible presentation choice requiring future
user participation is final density/wording polish during UI07; no functional UI04 decision remains blocked.
