==================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
==================================================

Закрыть подтверждённые функциональные разрывы между реализованным roadmap и FRS до того, как U31 начнёт только интеграционную проверку.

U30A не переписывает готовые системы. Он добавляет минимальные vertical slices поверх их canonical APIs:
1) canonical new game bootstrap;
2) normal save/load accessibility verification;
3) discovery of the AI state;
4) AI construction compliance;
5) minimal Stone Age military supply;
6) TPS aiming/attack using U20 rules;
7) continued play after victory.

If U26/U27 already fully implemented an item, verify and reuse it; do not duplicate it.

==================================================
2. CANONICAL NEW GAME
==================================================

Provide one normal player-facing/canonical New Game command and deterministic bootstrap from a selected/default world seed.

Mandatory initial state:
- exactly 10 player Characters;
- one valid player monarch and dynasty/family graph;
- one AI ruler/dynasty and viable AI population from U26;
- player and AI settlements on the U25 graph;
- separate owner-scoped resources, buildings, research and production;
- valid starting knowledge and building unlock state;
- no test-only mutation after bootstrap.

Same seed/config must reproduce stable IDs and logical initial hash according to the documented identity policy. Starting a second new game must dispose/rebind the old session and subscriptions rather than duplicate roots.

==================================================
3. NORMAL SAVE/LOAD
==================================================

Verify U27 exposes Save and Load through normal player UI. If still missing or incomplete, add the minimal UI/adapters now over the existing SaveCoordinator/providers.

Required:
- save a named/selected slot;
- advance/mutate normal gameplay;
- load and replace/rebind the session;
- preserve player/AI/dynasty/research/resources/buildings/units/map/events/supply;
- no duplicate callbacks/presenters/roots;
- clear provider error without exposing secrets.

Do not invent a second save format.

==================================================
4. AI DISCOVERY
==================================================

FRS MVP requires discovering the AI dynasty. Implement the smallest deterministic knowledge boundary, not a full fog-of-war system.

- At New Game the AI state's detailed identity/strength is unknown to the player unless starting config says otherwise.
- A canonical exploration/contact condition on the U25 graph reveals the AI settlement/state using stable IDs and a persisted discovered marker/tick.
- Discovery is exactly once, deterministic and survives save/load/off-camera catch-up.
- U27 Global UI hides undiscovered details and presents the discovery notification through existing UI/event surfaces.
- AI strategic simulation continues while undiscovered; hidden does not mean nonexistent.

No diplomacy, treaties or full visibility/terrain streaming.

==================================================
5. AI CONSTRUCTION COMPLIANCE
==================================================

Verify the U26 baseline AI creates at least one useful new building/site after New Game through canonical research, funding, placement/occupancy, construction and ownership rules.

If U26 only produces/researches against prebuilt content, fix the owning U26 integration rather than adding an U30A-only shortcut. Construction spends real AI resources, creates one canonical Building ID, joins the existing settlement and persists.

==================================================
6. MINIMAL STONE AGE MILITARY SUPPLY
==================================================

Implement a minimal data-driven supply model for MVP without a second inventory system:
- supply state belongs to the existing MilitaryUnit and references/consumes canonical resource inventories;
- Stone Age baseline requires food/rations and any ammunition/resource actually used by the current weapon catalog; fuel is a valid future/zero-requirement category, not fabricated consumption;
- travel and abstract/physical combat consume from one shared logical supply state at deterministic ticks;
- insufficient supply applies bounded data-driven movement/combat/morale modifiers through existing U25/U20/U23 hooks;
- no negative quantity, duplication or representation-mode refill;
- capture/destruction cannot duplicate supply;
- save/load and Physical↔Abstract preserve exact remaining supply/checkpoints.

Do not add strategic trade, convoy AI, roads or full logistics networks.

==================================================
7. TPS AIMING AND ATTACK
==================================================

Extend U22 direct control with the minimal FRS combat path:
- only eligible controlled monarch/heir/captain;
- pointer/mouse aiming and one explicit Attack input;
- uses the same U20 CombatService, CombatRules, weapon profile, target eligibility, cooldown, injury, health, death, morale and battle state;
- no second projectile/damage/health authority;
- other unit members continue their existing U20 AI orders while the captain is directly controlled;
- pause and x1/x2/x3/x5/x10 semantics remain correct;
- leaving TPS returns movement authority without duplicate attack or lost target/cooldown.

The current Stone Age melee profiles may use an aim/target ray and canonical melee-range validation. Do not invent firearms or later-era content. Presentation animation/VFX can remain placeholder.

==================================================
8. VICTORY CONTINUATION
==================================================

After canonical capture/defeat of the only AI state:
- record military victory exactly once;
- show a normal player-facing result/notification;
- continue in sandbox mode with clock, settlement, dynasty, production and save/load active;
- repeated load/advance does not re-award victory or freeze the campaign.

Player dynasty extinction remains the existing U17 GameOver rule.
